## substance

Half the increment is registry creation dates read over RDAP, 357,755 equivalent-English on 581,458
pairs, and they came from asking about names we invented rather than names we had found. Querying the
2.4 million undated names in our candidate pool returned zero in-window creation dates over 602
queries; sibling names, every `.com`, `.net` and `.org` label we hold re-suffixed to the other two,
returned 14,205 from 150,000. A registry can only date a name that survived.

Eleven sources are new, every one a machine-written record rather than a person's list. The largest
by volume is the 1999 RIPE database snapshot, used with the written permission of the RIPE NCC,
gratefully acknowledged, and the parser reads the domain name and nothing else. Section 2 of the
report lists all eleven with what dates one item and where the artifact is.
