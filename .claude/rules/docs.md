# Editing anything under docs/

- A page marked *generated* in `docs/index.md` is written by the script named there (CLAUDE.md,
  Files). Check that line before editing a page; the brief is regenerated from his originals.
- A new page gets one line in `docs/index.md`: what it is, and when to read it.
- A rule or a measured fact goes where `CLAUDE.md`, Files, puts it; a fact under its section of
  `docs/lore/laws.md`.
- Label an estimate in the sentence that carries its number; present nothing unmeasured as
  measured, and pad no list to reach a count.
